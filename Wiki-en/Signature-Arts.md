# ✦ Signature Arts & Forge Pedigree

Every alloy you forge into a weapon awakens a **Signature Art**: a named ability with its own mechanic and its own animation. The harder the alloy was to reach, the bigger the art — a composite of two common minerals plays a quick fusion, while a mythic prime takes over the sky.

Arts fire **on hit** (melee, arrow, bolt or thrown trident), on a per-player cooldown. When the weapon's ultimate fires on the same hit, the ultimate wins and the art waits for the next one — the two never stack.

---

## 🪜 The Forge Pedigree

A weapon's pedigree is the most demanding material in any of its parts.

| Tier | Pedigree | What it takes | Art | Chance | Cooldown | Reach | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | **Forged** | Brushed minerals and vanilla ores | — | — | — | — | — |
| II | **Alloyed** | Two minerals fused in the crucible | Fusion art | 15% | 8 s | 3 blocks | 55% of the hit |
| III | **Legendary** | One of the 16 curated recipes | Its own legendary art | 20% | 10 s | 4 blocks | 80% of the hit |
| IV | **Prime** | A legendary fused again with a mineral or a composite | The legendary art, ascended | 24% | 12 s | 5 blocks | 105% of the hit |
| V | **Mythic** | A legendary fused with a vanilla catalyst or a second legendary | Two arts back to back + finale | 28% | 14 s | 6 blocks | 130% of the hit |

The pedigree also scales the weapon's **ultimates**: every level past the first widens the spectacle by 12%, adds 5% damage, extra shockwave rings and denser particles. Prime and mythic ultimates put their name on the wielder's screen; a mythic ultimate also calls three thunderbolts and a column of light.

Legendary, prime and mythic weapons wear a **pedigree aura** while held: a slow ring in the art's colours at your feet, a star of light for primes and a crown for mythics.

## 🧪 Fusion Arts (Tier II)

A composite's art is read through **each of its two minerals on its own**, so every one of the **5,981 blendable pairs owns a different art**:

* **Name** — both minerals' [perk epithets](Perk-Names.md) plus the shape: Malachite + Ruby is *Verdant-Rubicund Fault*. No two pairs share a name.
* **Payload** (what it does) — the first mineral's character, its leading essence.
* **Shape** (what it looks like) — the second mineral's specialty, its most specific essence.
* **Colours** — the choreography is drawn in the first mineral's colour with the second one's as accent.
* **Trait echo** — every foe the art reaches also suffers **both minerals' own forge traits**: Malachite's Toxic Patina poisons, Ruby's Flame Edge ignites, Gravitite lifts, Sanguinite drains… at 60% of their direct-hit strength.

The crucible preview names the art and spells out both echoed traits before you fuse. The table below is the payload and shape vocabulary every fusion is built from:

| Essence | As payload | As shape |
|---|---|---|
| Infernal | **Searing** — ignites every foe for 5 s | **Pyre** — a ring of rising fire |
| Void | **Warping** — drags every foe to the centre | **Rift** — a rift implodes on the target |
| Primal | **Feral** — +25% raw impact | **Fang** — four claw-marks |
| Tempered | **Hardened** — Weakness II | **Anvil** — a phantom anvil falls |
| Radiant | **Blinding** — blindness and glowing | **Halo** — a descending halo |
| Resonant | **Echoing** — a second, softer pulse | **Chime** — three expanding chimes |
| Volatile | **Volatile** — a burning knockback blast | **Flare** — a radial flare |
| Terrain | **Earthen** — Slowness III | **Spire** — a circle of stone spires |
| Swift | **Galeborn** — Speed II and Haste II for you | **Gale** — a wind helix |
| Brutal | **Brutal** — +20% and a heavy knockback | **Fault** — a star of ground faults |
| Bulwark | **Stalwart** — Absorption and Resistance for you | **Ward** — a hexagonal ward around you |
| Ascendant | **Ascendant** — heals you | **Ascent** — a spiral of undying light |

## ✦ Prime Arts (Tier IV) and Mythic Arts (Tier V)

A prime keeps the art of the legendary it was fused from and plays it **ascended**: an ascension prelude (rings closing on the target and a pillar of light behind you), the legendary choreography at the prime's bigger scale, then an **overlay** chosen by the second ingredient:

* **A mineral or composite** → the partner's own word, payload and shape, plus its forge traits echoed on every foe — e.g. Cosmic Netherite + Ruby is `Gravity Collapse · Rubicund Ascension`, and its overlay sets foes alight with Ruby's Flame Edge.
* **A vanilla catalyst** (mythic) → a miniature of the catalyst's prime ultimate, e.g. `Silver Blizzard · Echo Shard Event Horizon` freezes, pulls, burns or blasts exactly like that ultimate.
* **A second legendary** (mythic) → the second legendary art plays right after the first, e.g. `Bell of the First Age ⨯ Thunderchain Conduit`.

Mythic arts end with a **finale**: three thunderbolts, four glowing obelisks rising out of the ground, a shockwave that rolls far past the fight and the art's name as a title on your screen.

A prime alloy's **trait** is now the name of its art, so two primes of the same legendary no longer share a trait. Every one of the 2,088 prime and mythic fusions of a legendary with a mineral, a catalyst or another legendary owns its own art name.

## 🔍 Reading an art before you forge it

* The **Alloy Crucible** previews the alloy two materials would fuse into — name, pedigree, signature art and, for primes, the ultimate and armor state — before the crucible is lit.
* The **Molds & Parts** preview shows the pedigree and art of the part you are about to cast.
* The **Weapon Assembly** perk preview adds the weapon's pedigree, its art and its proc line.
* Every alloy ingot and every forged weapon prints its art in the lore.

Assembling an alloy weapon makes the Forge answer as loud as its pedigree; a mythic weapon is announced with a title.
