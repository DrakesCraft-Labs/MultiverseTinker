# 🏷️ Perk Names — How a forged piece gets its name

Every weapon, tool and armor piece prints a **signature perk** in its lore: the mechanic of its type
plus the name of the minerals it was forged from. This page is the complete list of those names and the
rule that builds them.

> ⚙️ **The rule in one line:** each part contributes the **epithet** of its own mineral, in forge order,
> ahead of the type's mechanic: `<head epithet> <handle epithet> <binding epithet> <mechanic>`.
>
> 🧾 **In game:** the **Alloy Codex** prints a mineral's epithet on its own entry — in the Mineral
> Catalog, the Combination Explorer, the Legendary Recipes and the Prime Catalysts — so the word can be
> read before anything is blended.

## 📖 A worked example

Two swords of the same type, same tier, same head and handle — only the pommel changes:

| Forged from | Perk name in the lore |
|---|---|
| Borax · Amethyst · **Gold** | **Fluxforged Resonant Auric** Sweeping Cleave |
| Borax · Amethyst · **Diamond** | **Fluxforged Resonant Adamant** Sweeping Cleave |
| Borax · Amethyst · **Iron** | **Fluxforged Resonant Ironclad** Sweeping Cleave |

The mechanic (*Sweeping Cleave*) is the same because the sword type is the same; the name tells you the
**whole build**, so two swords can never be mistaken for each other in a chest.

> 🧠 **Why the minerals and not just the essence?** An earlier version named the perk after the essence of
> the head mineral alone. That broke down exactly here: **Amethyst and Diamond teach the same essences**
> (`Primal`, `Terrain`, `Radiant`), so both swords printed the very same name even though they were made
> of different minerals. The epithet of every mineral is unique, so a name now identifies one build.

## 🔢 The 139 epithets

One word per registered mineral, alloy and catalyst. **None of them repeats**, which is what guarantees
two different combinations can never share a perk name.

## ⚗ Alloys forged at the crucible

A composite or a prime does not have its own word, because it does not exist until a player blends it.
It borrows the words of the minerals it was built from, which keeps the name unique by construction:

| Forged | Perk epithet |
|---|---|
| Tin + Zinc (`mvtink_alloy_tin_zinc`) | **Malleable-Galvanized** |
| Nether Star + Manyullyn (`mvtink_prime_...`) | **Prime Starforged** |

## 🧩 What the epithets do *not* change

* The **mechanic** still comes from the equipment type: `Sweeping Cleave`, `Infused Volley`,
  `Vein Resonance`, `Kinetic Dampener`…
* The **effects** still come from the **essences**. The epithet names the build; the head part's essence
  decides what the perk *does*, and the `Essence Focus` row in the lore still prints it with its
  concentration percentage.
* The **trait rows** are unchanged: they are labelled with the moment they fire (`on sweep`, `while
  mining`, `when struck`…).
