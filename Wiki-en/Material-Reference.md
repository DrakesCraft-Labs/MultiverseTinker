# 🧩 Material Reference — How to obtain every material

This page says, material by material, **where it comes from** and **what it gives you**: its source, its class and rarity, its forge statistics, its trait (on weapons, tools and armor) and its **essences**. For the full mixing recipes (all 5,995 pairs) see the [Alloy Recipe Index](Alloy-Recipe-Index.md).

## 🧭 Where each category comes from

| Category | How it is obtained |
|---|---|
| **Overworld** | Prospector Brush on Overworld geology (Stone / Cobblestone). |
| **The Nether** | Prospector Brush on Nether geology (Netherrack / Blackstone). |
| **The End** | Prospector Brush on End geology (End Stone). |
| **Vanilla Ores** | Refined from vanilla Minecraft ores (smeltery + casting cauldron). |
| **Legendary alloys** | Alloy Crucible: blend the two parent materials listed in each recipe. |
| **Catalysts** | Real vanilla items (Nether Star, Blue Ice…) dropped in the crucible next to a legendary alloy. |
| **Composites / Primes** | Forged in the crucible from a valid pair (see the Recipe Index). Saved to `dynamic-alloys.yml`. |
