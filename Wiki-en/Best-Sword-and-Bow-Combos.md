# 🗡️ Best Sword and Bow Combinations

A practical forging guide: what to put in each part to forge the best modular broadsword and the best modular longbow. The tables below are the **prime era**: the piece that decides identity (the sword's head, the bow's limbs) is a **prime alloy**, the top of the anvil's ladder.

> 📐 **How the numbers are produced.** They come from the anvil's own math: every item is forged and its stats are read back from the lore, so they are exactly what you will see in game.
> * **Sword (3 parts)**: `Damage = 4.0 + attack(Head/Blade) + attack(Pommel/Guard) / 3` · `Durability = Handle + Head + Pommel / 2`
> * **Bow (2 parts)**: `Damage = 4.0 + attack(Limbs) + attack(String) / 2` · `Durability = Limbs + String`
> * The **head mineral (blade/limbs) decides the identity**: its essence and trait open the perk's name with their epithets ([full list](Perk-Names.md)).
> * **Essence Focus** is what the lore prints as the share of the weapon that carries that essence. At **≥80%** the essence's **cinematic ultimate** unlocks (Infernal → *Meteor Storm*, Void → *Singularity Collapse*, Terrain → *Earthen Grasp*…).
> * **Tier evolution does not change the order.** Wood → Stone → Copper → Iron → Gold → Diamond → Netherite only adds flat damage and durability to every row below, so the optimal combination is the same in every era.
> * **Why the ceiling is a prime.** A **catalyst** carries no metallurgical mass: fusing one with a legendary alloy builds the fusion from that legendary alone, so the whole fusion bonus lands on its own stats. That is the sharpest — and among the toughest — mineral the anvil can make. The identity still comes from the legendary; the catalyst only decides the **weapon ultimate** and the **armor state**.
> * **How to read the tables.** They are ranked by **damage**, and the **Essence** column also carries the Essence Focus each forge ships with (a 50% row hits just as hard, but has not unlocked its ultimate yet). The **last row** does not compete on damage: it is *the toughest forge in the catalog*, listed as a staying-power reference.

### The sword's golden rule
1. **Head = identity.** Pick the prime alloy of the legendary you want: it keeps that legendary's trait (*Cosmic Gravity*, *Abyssal Cleave*, *Insatiable*…). The fusion's catalyst does not change the stats, only the ultimate and the armor state.
2. **Handle = durability and focus.** It is the part that contributes full durability (undivided) and the one that, sharing the head's essence, pushes Essence Focus higher.
3. **Pommel = damage.** It is the only part that adds `/3` of attack, so spend your most aggressive mineral there.
4. **Spread alloys across all three parts.** Legendary traits stack, and every part adds its epithet to the perk's compound name (a forge of three different alloys reads three epithets at once).

### The bow's golden rule
1. **Limbs = identity**: the prime alloy on the limbs sets the arrows' essence and opens the perk's name (*Infused Volley*).
2. **String = durability and focus**: its durability counts in full and its attack counts at `/2`, so the right string barely costs damage and still adds staying power.
3. **Unlike the sword**, a bow has only two parts: there is no third slot to recover lost damage, so every attack point you give up on the string shows from the first shot.

## 🧪 How to verify your own combination

* **In game**: open the **Alloy Codex** (the book button on the *Alloy Crucible* tab) and use the **Combination Explorer** to list every valid partner of a mineral.
* **In the browser**: the [interactive alloy explorer](https://drakescraft-labs.github.io/MultiverseTinker/) lists every material with its forge stats and essences, and its two-material crucible shows the name, id, durability, speed, damage and inherited essences of the prime any pair would forge — without spending an ingot. Its build calculator prices the same broadsword and longbow from their parts and tier before you forge them, so a combination can be checked against the numbers on this page first.
* **In the weapon's lore**: the `✦ Weapon Perk` and `• Essence Focus` lines tell you the dominant essence and the percentage; at **100%** you know the ultimate is guaranteed.
* **Forge a prime legendary**: fuse your favourite legendary alloy with one of the **12 vanilla catalysts** in the crucible; the catalyst decides the ultimate and the armor state, and that fusion is the most powerful head of that identity.
* **Raise the tier**: wood → stone → copper → iron → gold → diamond → netherite only adds flat damage and durability, so the optimal combination is the same in every era.
